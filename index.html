I / Foundations

Different beginnings.
Shared discoveries.

One home for both settings, with their own maps and histories kept intact. The atlas connects the stories through a common type system, temporal mysteries, Pokémon evolution, and exploration.

The heritage atlas

Oravel / Oraveth

The original hyper-region records: Cendara, Khorvath, Mirellune and the other great continental territories. Home to the Oravel Heartsea, Lydmere Crown, Echobell Shores and the foundations of the 50-type system.

The reimagined atlas

Vaeloria

The newer planetary Hyper-Region, divided into twelve continents and layered with Mega-Regions, Wild Zones, expedition territories, and intergalactic frontiers. The world's strange past sometimes returns without warning.

IV / Featured geographical anomaly

Where yesterday
still flows.

Haldenreach · The Continent of Endless Waterways

The Receding Past

Across its immense freshwater basins, ancient riverbeds reappear at certain hours. Modern rivers briefly reconnect with vanished lakes; prehistoric freshwater Pokémon return to habitats erased long ago. The Sevenfold Watershed and the seas of Istramar and Velanthis anchor a civilization shaped by water and time.

Regional progression rule

Eight Gyms to begin. Four more each Mega-Region.

In each continent's main challenge progression, Mega-Region 1 starts with 8 Gyms, Mega-Region 2 has 12, Mega-Region 3 has 16, and so on. This tool shows the expected total for one Mega-Region; you can still record custom circuits and individual Gyms below.
